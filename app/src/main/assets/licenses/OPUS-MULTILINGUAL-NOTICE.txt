OPUS-MT translation models

Model authors: Language Technology Research Group, University of Helsinki.
OPUS-MT: Jorg Tiedemann and Santhosh Thottingal, EAMT 2020.
https://opus.nlpl.eu/
https://github.com/Helsinki-NLP/Opus-MT

The app downloads ONNX conversions published by Xenova and onnx-community.
These are converted and encoder-quantized versions of the original weights.
The app uses an INT8 encoder and FP32 decoder. Models are separate optional
downloads; their files, source revisions and checksums are listed in the
translation-models.json catalog. Model quality varies by language.

The Portuguese option selects the >>pt<< target token of the multilingual
Romance model. The Chinese option selects >>cmn_Hans<< (Simplified Chinese).
The recognition-to-English speech translation feature is supplied by Whisper,
not by OPUS-MT. See whisper-MIT.txt for Whisper's license.
